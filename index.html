FAQ

Questions, answered

What is GapHunter?

GapHunter is a free directory of app, service and web-tool ideas where demand is high but good solutions are still rare. Each idea comes with a problem statement, MVP plan, monetization models and a 7-day validation checklist.

How is the Gap Score calculated?

The Gap Score (0–100) is an editorial estimate that weighs how strong the demand is against how much supply and competition already exists, plus how timely the idea is. It is a prioritisation aid, not verified market data — always validate before building.

How often are new ideas added?

New ideas are researched and added every Monday. Existing ideas are re-checked too: when competition grows, an idea moves from Early to Hot to Crowded.

Can I build one of these ideas?

Yes. Every idea is free to use. Pick one that fits your skills and budget, run the 7-day validation checklist, and launch the MVP features listed on its page.

Why focus on 2027 and beyond?

The best returns go to people who build before a market gets crowded. We look for new technology, rule changes and behaviour shifts that open gaps over the next 12–24 months.
